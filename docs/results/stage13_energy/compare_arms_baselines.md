# Stage 13 corrector arms against GraphCast and ClimODE (2020)

Latitude-weighted RMSE in physical units, means over the 31 initialisations of the GraphCast protocol in 2020 (every 12 days, 00 UTC; held out for every model of ours). GraphCast = the WeatherBench-2 forecast coarsened to 5.625 deg. ClimODE = the published numbers of ClimODE's Table 6 (Verma, Heinonen & Garg, ICLR 2024; WeatherBench 5.625 deg, test 2017-2018), so other years and leads to 36 h only. Generated by `scripts/report_baselines.py`. E2 = memory model (D-noactor) + energy-neutral SFNO corrector under the E1 bound; E2mlp = the same with a per-column 1x1 corrector (local control); E3 = soft energy penalty, no projection, no bound. warm 30 d = 30-day re-observed ERA5 spin-up before each initialisation; cold = empty memory. N1 cold = the stage 9 physics model.
